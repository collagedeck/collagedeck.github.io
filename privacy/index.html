CollageDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use CollageDeck, a photo and video collage app for iPhone. CollageDeck is made by Mehmet Ali Akbay ("I", "me", "we"), who is responsible for the processing described here. You can reach me at mehmetaliakbay@outlook.com.

In short

  • Your photos, videos and projects stay on your iPhone. CollageDeck has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never uploads your photos, videos or projects.
  • If you buy CollageDeck Pro, as a subscription or as Lifetime Pro, Apple handles the payment, and RevenueCat, the purchase service CollageDeck uses, processes your purchase records.
  • If CollageDeck crashes or hits an error, a crash report goes to Sentry, the crash reporting service CollageDeck uses, unless you turn this off. Crash reports never contain your photos, videos, projects or anything you typed.
  • No ads, no tracking across other companies' apps or websites, no analytics service, and no selling of data.

1. Photos and videos

  • Choosing photos. CollageDeck asks for access to your photo library so you can pick photos and videos inside the app. You can allow access to all of your library or only to items you select, or decline and use the system photo picker instead, which lets you choose items without giving CollageDeck access to your library. You can change this at any time in the iPhone Settings app, under Privacy & Security → Photos.
  • Private copies. When you use a photo or video in a collage, CollageDeck keeps a copy of it inside its own storage on your iPhone, so your project keeps working even if the original changes. CollageDeck never changes or deletes anything in your photo library.
  • Metadata. Photo copies are re-encoded, and the information embedded in the original (such as camera details or where it was taken) is not kept. Video copies are exact copies of the file and keep whatever the video contains, which can include where it was recorded. These copies stay on your iPhone; CollageDeck doesn't read or use location information.
  • Saving to Photos. When you save a finished collage, CollageDeck asks for permission to add items to your photo library, and uses it only to save what you export.
  • Cutouts. Lifting a subject from its background happens on your iPhone, with Apple's built-in Vision framework. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • CollageDeck doesn't use the camera, the microphone or location services.

2. Projects and other data on your iPhone

  • Your projects (their layouts, the text you type, drawings, stickers and other edits), the private copies of their photos and videos, and their previews are stored in CollageDeck's storage on your iPhone.
  • Your preferences are stored there too: appearance, haptics, favorite designs, layouts and fonts, recent colors and stickers, the last export settings, the optional signature you can add to exports, whether crash reports are on, and whether you have seen the introduction and the editor's tips.
  • None of this is sent to me or to anyone else. There is no account and no cloud sync.
  • Backups. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, the backup can include CollageDeck's data, as it does for other apps. Apple handles those backups under its own terms.
  • Exports. An exported image or video is made on your iPhone, then saved to Photos or handed to the share sheet, as you choose; CollageDeck deletes its temporary copy. What you share is then handled by the app or service you share it with.
  • Free template exports. CollageDeck counts how many times you have exported a collage made from a ready-made design without CollageDeck Pro. The count is a single number, kept in two places on your iPhone: the iOS Keychain and CollageDeck's storage. It says nothing about which collage you exported, holds no photos or project content, and is never sent to me or to anyone else.

3. CollageDeck Pro purchases

  • Apple. CollageDeck Pro is bought with your Apple Account through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; I never see your payment details. Apple's handling of your information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat. CollageDeck uses RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) to show plans and prices, confirm purchases, restore them and know whether Pro is active. The RevenueCat software in CollageDeck sends RevenueCat:
    • your App Store purchase records, such as the product, the purchase date and transaction identifiers, and for a subscription its renewal and expiry dates;
    • a random anonymous identifier that the software creates on your iPhone;
    • technical information it needs to answer, such as the app version, iOS version, device type, your country or region, language and currency, and your IP address as part of the connection.
    RevenueCat also receives notices about these purchases directly from Apple, such as renewals, cancellations and refunds. CollageDeck doesn't give RevenueCat your name, email address or any account, because it has none. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Crash reports

CollageDeck uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc., doing business as Sentry, United States) to receive reports when the app crashes or hits an error, so problems can be found and fixed.

A crash report contains:

  • the kind of error and a short error message, with file names, file locations, web addresses and identifiers removed;
  • where in CollageDeck's own code it happened;
  • your iPhone model, iOS version, language setting, available memory and storage, CollageDeck's version, and whether CollageDeck was on screen at the time;
  • a random identifier that the Sentry software creates the first time CollageDeck runs, used to count how many installations a problem affects. It isn't linked to your name, email address, Apple Account or advertising identifier, and it is deleted when you delete CollageDeck;
  • the last few things you did in CollageDeck, as simple labels and numbers, for example "export started, PNG, HD" or "editor opened, 3 photos".

A crash report never contains your photos, videos, project contents, the text you typed, file names, screenshots of the app or recordings of your screen.

Sentry receives your IP address as part of the connection; CollageDeck's Sentry project is set not to store it. Sentry stores crash reports in the United States and keeps them for up to 90 days. See the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Turning crash reports off. Crash reports are on when you first install CollageDeck. To turn them off, go to Settings → Preferences in CollageDeck and turn off "Share anonymous usage". Error reports stop at once; reports of crashes in the app's native code stop the next time you open CollageDeck.

5. Other connections

  • CollageDeck doesn't connect to any server of mine; there isn't one.
  • The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Support links in CollageDeck open pages of this website in an in-app browser. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (GitHub General Privacy Statement). The website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.
  • If your photos are stored in iCloud Photos, iOS may download the original from iCloud when you choose it in CollageDeck. That happens between your iPhone and Apple.

6. What CollageDeck doesn't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifier.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites, and no data brokers.
  • No analytics service. The usage events CollageDeck records are only kept as the short list of recent actions in a crash report.
  • No selling or sharing of personal information for advertising.
  • No account or login.

7. Keeping and deleting data

  • On your iPhone. Projects stay until you delete them. Deleting a project also deletes its private copies of photos and videos and its previews; CollageDeck offers Undo for a few seconds first. You can remove your export signature in Settings. Deleting CollageDeck from your iPhone deletes its projects, photo and video copies, previews and preferences, apart from backups you made and anything you saved to Photos or shared. One item can remain: iOS may keep the free-export count in the Keychain after CollageDeck is deleted, so it is still there if you install CollageDeck again. It is a single number and identifies nothing about you.
  • Crash reports are deleted by Sentry after the retention period above.
  • Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat for as long as needed to provide CollageDeck Pro, and by Apple under its own policies.
  • Asking about your data. CollageDeck has no accounts, so I usually can't tell which crash report or purchase record belongs to you. If you'd like to ask about, receive or delete such data, contact me at mehmetaliakbay@outlook.com. The order ID on your Apple purchase receipt can help find a purchase record.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the European Union, the United Kingdom or Türkiye (Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, "KVKK"), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you, to restrict or object to its processing, to receive it in a portable form, and to complain to a data protection authority. Contact me to use these rights.

Legal grounds: processing your purchases is necessary to provide the CollageDeck Pro you buy; crash reports rely on the legitimate interest in keeping CollageDeck working and secure, and you can object at any time by turning them off as described above.

9. International transfers

RevenueCat and Sentry are based in the United States, and your information may be processed there or in other countries where they or their providers operate. These transfers rely on the providers' data processing terms, including standard contractual clauses where required.

10. Children

CollageDeck is a general audience app. It isn't directed to children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The information described above doesn't identify who is using the app.

11. Changes to this policy
