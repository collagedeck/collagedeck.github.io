CollageDeck Support

Something not working, or a question? Write to mehmetaliakbay@outlook.com.

To help me help you, include:

  • your iPhone model and iOS version (Settings → General → About);
  • CollageDeck's version, shown at the bottom of CollageDeck's Settings;
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead.

A screenshot can help; please don't send private photos you'd rather keep to yourself.

Questions

Where are my projects stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside CollageDeck's storage. There is no account and no cloud sync, so deleting CollageDeck deletes your projects. An iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone can include them. Save or share the collages you want to keep.

CollageDeck can't see my photos.

Open the iPhone Settings app → Privacy & Security → Photos → CollageDeck, and choose Full Access, or Limited Access and the photos you want to use. You can also tap "Use the system picker" in CollageDeck to choose photos without giving access to your library.

Saving to Photos doesn't work.

CollageDeck needs permission to add photos: Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → CollageDeck. Also check that your iPhone has free storage. You can always use Share to send the collage elsewhere.

Does CollageDeck change or delete my photos?

No. CollageDeck keeps its own private copy of the photos and videos you use, and deleting a project deletes only that copy.

Is there a watermark?

No, never, on free or Pro exports.

What does CollageDeck Pro include?

Unlimited exports of collages made from ready-made designs. Everything else is already free: every design, layout, pattern, Cutout and export size, with no watermark. Pro is available as a weekly or yearly subscription, or as Lifetime Pro, a one-time purchase; prices are shown in the app.

How many free exports do I get?

Collages made from a layout export without limit. A collage made from a ready-made design can be exported 3 times for free on each iPhone; the Export screen shows how many are left. An export counts once its file is made, even if you then close the share sheet; a cancelled or failed export doesn't count. After the third, exporting a design needs CollageDeck Pro.

Why did my free exports reset, or not reset, after reinstalling?

The count lives only on your iPhone, in two places: CollageDeck's storage, which goes when you delete the app, and the iOS Keychain, which iOS usually keeps after an app is deleted. So reinstalling on the same iPhone normally keeps the count, though Apple doesn't guarantee it. Restoring a backup onto the same iPhone brings back that backup's count; a new iPhone starts at 3 unless it was set up from a backup of the old one. With Pro the count doesn't matter.

How do I restore CollageDeck Pro on a new iPhone, or after reinstalling?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Restore purchases in CollageDeck's Settings or on the CollageDeck Pro screen. This works for an active subscription and for Lifetime Pro. Pro belongs to the Apple Account that bought it: it can be restored on any iPhone signed in to that Apple Account, but it can't be moved to another Apple Account, store account or platform.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the iPhone Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → CollageDeck → Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before it renews. If you have Pro, CollageDeck's Settings also has Manage subscription. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription. Lifetime Pro is paid once, so there's nothing to cancel; if you buy it while you have a subscription, cancel the subscription so it doesn't renew.

How does the free trial work?

If a subscription plan offers a free trial and you are eligible, CollageDeck shows how long it lasts and the date you'll be charged. If you allow notifications, CollageDeck reminds you before the trial ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before it ends to avoid being charged.

Can I get a refund?

Subscriptions and Lifetime Pro are bought through the App Store, so Apple handles refunds under its policies. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What are crash reports, and can I turn them off?

When CollageDeck crashes or hits an error, it sends a short technical report so the problem can be fixed. Reports never contain your photos, videos, projects or text. To turn them off, open CollageDeck's Settings → Preferences and turn off "Share anonymous usage". The Privacy Policy has the details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete projects in CollageDeck, or delete CollageDeck from your iPhone to remove its projects, copies and preferences. iOS may keep one number, the free-export count, in the Keychain; see the Privacy Policy. For crash reports or purchase records, see Keeping and deleting data.

Which iPhones does CollageDeck work on?

iPhones with iOS 16.4 or later.
